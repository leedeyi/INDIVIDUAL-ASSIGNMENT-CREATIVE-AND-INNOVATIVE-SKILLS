Conclusion

By studying the National Zoo of Malaysia, we have learned that running a successful organization requires balancing modern technology with good physical maintenance. Although implementing online ticketing, expanding the tram service, and collaborating with local food vendors help to handle more visitors and enhance visitor convenience, the neglect of facility maintenance and the dirtiness of fences still damage the zoo's reputation. Ultimately, the National Zoo tells us that digital upgrades must be combined with high standards of daily cleaning, safety, and operational management in order to truly win the trust of customers.
