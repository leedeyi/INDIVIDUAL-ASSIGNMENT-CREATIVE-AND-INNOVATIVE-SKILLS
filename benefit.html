Benefits

Financial Recovery and Stability

The implementation of the 'Adopt An Animal' initiatives played a crucial supporting role during the zoo's financial crisis especially during the closure periods. This initiatives successfully raised over 1 million ringgit in donations from the public and business community. These funds were used to cover the daily animal feed, medical suppliers and veterinary care costs, ensuring the continuity of operations while safeguarding animal welfare.

Increased Conversation Awareness

Through the transparency of "Adopt An Animal", the local community gained a clearer understanding of the actual financial costs and efforts involved in protecting wild animals. The public's knowledge of the high operating expenses successfully made Malaysians pay more attention to the issue of wildlife protection, shifting the public's mindset from passive consumers of entertainment to active partners in preserving nature.

Upgraded Food Quality and Diverse Food Options

By collaborating with certified local business, the zoo guarantees hygienic, high-quality and reasonably priced meal choices to meet the diverse taste preference of tourists. Through a structured and centralized catering model, the zoo can implement strict hygiene standards and quality control measures for all its dining areas. As a result, visitors can enjoy a more satisfactory dining environment, which leads to an extension of their stay in the park and an increase in the overall consumption level of visitors.

Improving animal welfare and strict safety standards

Conducting daily inspections of the electric fence and tree branches, and mandating that keepers receive training significantly reduces safety risks, creating a safe environment for both animals and visitors. Proactive infrastructure maintenance such as twice a day inspections of high-risk fences for large carnivores helps ensure the safety of the fences and prevent animals from escaping or accidents from occurring to visitors. Combining these physical measures with weekly mandatory retraining courses and certified annual safety plans enables keepers to effectively respond to emergencies, thereby raising the overall operational standards of the zoo to the advanced level of international animal welfare.
