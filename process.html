Process

Analyzing visitor feedback, social media reports and financial documents that negatively affect the Zoo Negara Malaysia reputation. For example, the core issues that faced by Zoo Negara Malaysia included financial stress caused by high monthly animal food and operational costs, visitors' perception regarding the inadequate barriers and uneven walkways when viewing panels.

To uncover the root causes of these issues, Zoo Negara evaluated the qualitative and quantitative data in key operational areas. By categorizing public visitor evaluations and social media comments, we identified specific patterns of dissatisfaction, particularly in the hygiene conditions of animal enclosures and the lack of staff supervision in enforcing standard operating procedures (SOP) at the entrances. Additionally, we reviewed past financial records to examine the constraints related to budget limitations and resource allocation.

Generating a wide range of potential solutions for both short-term and long-term strategies. Short-term ideas focused on practical maintenance such as increasing daily cleaning schedules for supervise glass and aquatic pools, inspecting electric safety fences twice daily and strengthening entrance SOP checks. While long-term ideas focused on financial sustainability and upgrading physical infrastructure.

The proposed solutions were evaluated based on feasibility, cost-effectiveness and impact. All the measures have been evaluated to determine their feasibility within the zoo's current budget and staffing capacity of the zoo. At the same time, prioritizing actions that can most directly restore tourists' confidence, enhance safety, and maintain Malaysia's international image have been given priority.

The most practical measures and high impact measures were integrated into a comprehensive action. This comprehensive strategy integrates immediate facility maintenance and safety management with long-term financial planning and facility modernization, providing a sustainable development path for Zoo Negara Malaysia.
