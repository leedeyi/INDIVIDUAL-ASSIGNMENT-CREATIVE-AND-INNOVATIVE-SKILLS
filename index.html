ZOO NEGARA MALAYSIA

Zoo Negara Malaysia was officially opened on 14th November 1963 by Malaysia's first Prime Minister, Tunku Abdul Rahman. This worldwide popular zoo has since been managed by the Malaysian Zoological Society (MZS), a non-governmental organization established to create the first local zoo for Malaysians. Our zoo has a total of over 3,027 specimens from 327 species of mammals, birds, reptiles, amphibians, and fish. Encompassing 61.53 acres of land, making it the biggest zoo in Malaysia. Located just 5 kilometers from Kuala Lumpur's city centre, Zoo Negara is open daily from 9 AM until 5 PM, with the last admission at 4 p.m.
